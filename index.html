The plain version

Sell your house fast in Montgomery or Prince George's County, Maryland.

Most people who call us are not selling a house. They are trying to stop paying for one. A parent's place that needs a lot of work. A rental that stopped being worth the grief. A house that has sat empty while the taxes and the insurance kept running.

We buy it as it stands, with our own money. You do not fix it, you do not empty it, and you do not pay a commission.

Who you are dealing with

We are the buyer, not the broker.

Dofrane Acquisitions is the buyer. Not a lead broker, not a referral service, not an agent hunting a listing. We are a Maryland investor. We sign the contract in our own name, we put up the deposit, and we take the house the way it stands today.

Two things you should know before you talk to anyone in this business, including us.

We are not a real estate broker or agent. We do not represent you, we do not list your house, and we do not charge you a commission. We are on the other side of the table. Read anything we hand you as if that is true, because it is.

We may assign the contract to another buyer instead of closing on it ourselves. On an owner occupied house of four units or fewer, Maryland law requires that you be told this in writing before you sign, and if you were not told, you can cancel any time before closing with no penalty and get your deposit position back. That is Md. Code, Real Property § 10-715. We say it in the first conversation, not on page nine.

When this is the right call

Seven houses we say yes to.

A

It needs a lot of work and you are not putting more money into it

Roof, systems, foundation, a kitchen from a different decade. On a listing, that money leaves your pocket first and you hope it comes back at settlement. Here it comes out of the price, and you never front it.

B

You cannot face emptying it

Fifty years of things, and you are the one who has to decide what happens to every one of them. You do not have to. Take what you want to keep and leave the rest where it sits: the furniture, the closets, the basement, the garage, the shed. We are not going to ask you to clear it, and we are not going to charge you for it either.

C

It has been sitting empty and the bills have not stopped

Taxes, insurance, the grass, and a policy that gets complicated the moment nobody lives there. An empty house also collects the kind of attention nobody wants. If the estate is still open, say so on the form, so the timeline gets built around the court's calendar instead of ours.

D

You do not live near it

Every decision is a phone call, a favour asked of a neighbour, or a drive you do not have a free weekend for.

E

You are done being a landlord

Turnover, deferred repairs, a lease you did not write. Tell us the occupancy and the lease on the form. It changes the number. It does not change whether we look.

F

It has been listed for months and nothing is happening

The price came down once already and the showings go nowhere. If you are still inside a listing agreement, say so; we will not step around it.

G

The property has code or permit problems

Open violations, unpermitted work, a citation taped to the door. Say it plainly. It goes into the number and it does not scare us off.

When you should not sell to us

A clean house with time on the clock belongs with an agent.

The most common thing sellers say about buyers like us is that we pay less than the house is worth. That is correct. We are not going to argue with it.

If the house is in decent shape, on a street where things sell, and you have two or three months to wait, list it with an agent. After the commission you will almost always walk away with more money than we can pay.

That is not modesty, it is arithmetic. We have to buy at a price that leaves room for the repairs and for the next buyer's margin. An agent does not carry either of those, so on a clean house with time on the clock, an agent wins.

Call us when the condition, the timing, or the situation makes that trade a bad one. That is the entire business.

We also do not assume you are stuck. Plenty of people who fill in this form are tired rather than desperate, and a tired person with a sound house should list it. And before you sign anything with anyone in this business, us included, ask them to show you they can actually pay. If they will not show you, do not sign.

Questions people actually ask

The five that come up every time.

Do I need to fix anything before you look at it?

No. As-is means we price the house in the condition it is in on the day we walk it, and we do not come back with a repair credit after an inspection. You are not expected to paint, patch, haul anything away, or empty the basement. If something is broken, tell us on the form so it is in the number from the start instead of showing up as a surprise later.

What does as-is actually mean on your contract?

It means we accept the physical condition. It does not erase what Maryland requires you to disclose: on most single family residential sales of four units or fewer, the seller still delivers the Real Property § 10-702 disclosure or disclaimer statement. As-is is about repairs, not about hiding what you know. We would rather hear the bad news early, because a number that moves after a walkthrough is how people end up feeling worked over.

Do I pay anything?

No commission, because nobody is acting as an agent in the sale. No listing fee, no marketing budget, no cleanout invoice. Ordinary settlement costs still exist, and Maryland's default is that recordation and transfer taxes are shared equally between the two sides unless the contract says otherwise, Md. Code, Real Property § 14-104. Whatever the split ends up being, it will be in writing before you sign.

Will you record our call?

Only if you say yes. Maryland requires every person on a call to agree before it can be recorded, Md. Code, Courts and Judicial Proceedings § 10-402. We ask at the start of the first call. If you say no, the call carries on exactly the same and nothing is recorded.

The house is listed with an agent right now. Can I still send this?

Send it, and say so on the form. If you are inside a listing agreement, your agreement governs and we will not step around it or ask you to. In most cases the right move is to talk to your agent first. We will tell you that instead of pretending the agreement is not there.
