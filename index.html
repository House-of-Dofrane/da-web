What you do not have to do

Three things you can stop thinking about.

You do not empty it.

Furniture, boxes, a basement that has been filling for forty years. Take what you want. Leave the rest exactly where it is. Clearing it out is our problem, not yours.

You do not fix it.

The roof, the systems, the water that came in last spring. We price the house as it stands today. No inspection list, no repair credits, no work before closing.

You get a number, not a maybe.

The offer comes in writing, with the figure on it and how we arrived at it. If it does not work for you, say no and you will not hear from us again.
