**Subject:** Wholesale real estate landing page, DC/MD/VA, four questions before launch

---

We are launching a lead-generation page for Dofrane Acquisitions, a real estate acquisitions
business operating in the District of Columbia, Maryland and Virginia. Sellers submit a property
address and a phone number. We then make a cash offer in writing. Some of those we close ourselves
and some we assign to an end buyer, decided deal by deal.

The page is built and staged at a private preview URL. It is not public and the domain still points
elsewhere. I can send you the link and a PDF of every published word.

Four questions.

**1. Licensing across the three jurisdictions.**
We advertise in all three and expect to close a majority in Maryland. Virginia Code 54.1-2100(iii)
appears to treat a pattern of contract assignments as brokerage activity. At what point does
advertising to, and taking assignment fees from, Virginia or District of Columbia sellers require a
license in that jurisdiction, and does the answer change if the Virginia and DC deals are closed by
double closing rather than assignment?

**2. Assignment disclosure in Maryland.**
Maryland Real Property 10-715. What exactly must be disclosed, in what document, and at what point,
when a contract to purchase a residential property is assigned. Does anything have to appear on the
advertising itself, or only in the contract? Our current footer line reads:

> An offer made through this page may involve the assignment of a contract for the purchase of the
> property. Any such assignment is disclosed in writing before settlement.

**3. The call to action.**
The two buttons on the page read "Get Offer" and "Get Cash Offer." A seller who clicks has not been
underwritten, so we cannot guarantee an offer follows every submission. Is that wording a problem
under Maryland or Virginia consumer protection or deceptive advertising rules, and if so what
wording would you use instead?

**4. The disclosure block as drafted.**
Six lines sit in the footer, each in its own element so any of them can be replaced in one edit.
Are they correct, are any of them wrong, and what is missing?

- An offer made through this page may involve the assignment of a contract for the purchase of the
  property. Any such assignment is disclosed in writing before settlement.
- Dofrane Acquisitions is not a real estate broker and does not provide brokerage services. We do not
  represent you in the sale of your property.
- An offer made here is not an appraisal and is not a valuation of your property.
- Calls are recorded only with the consent of all parties to the call.
- An offer made here does not affect a seller's disclosure obligations.
- We do business in accordance with federal fair housing law.

For context on what we have deliberately kept off the page: there is no pre-foreclosure or
behind-on-payments angle anywhere, on the view that Maryland Real Property 7-301 and following
creates exposure we do not want; there is no divorce angle, because marital status is a protected
class; there are no testimonials, review scores or deal counts, because we have none we can
substantiate; and the page collects a phone number with a single unticked consent box whose exact
sentence, timestamp and originating page we log with the submission. We do not send SMS at all yet.

What we need back is a yes or no on each of the four, and any line you want changed. Happy to get on
a call if that is faster.
