# COPY_RATIONALE, dofraneacquisitions.com rebuild

Package `2026-09-08-da-site-rebuild`. Every line of visible copy, the seller pain it is rooted in, and
the keyword it serves. Written under R-02: **House of Dofrane is the investor, the page speaks to the
owner of the house.**

**Every headline now traces twice:** to an approved-or-provisional keyword, and to a numbered phrase in
`SWIPE_FILE.md`, which holds 29 verbatim phrases captured from 107 comments across 10 real threads. A
line with a `#n` below is rooted in that phrase. A line with no `#n` is my judgment and is marked as
such, because the difference is the whole point of the exercise.

**Provisional marker.** Tier 1 keywords come from `ads/keyword_research_montgomery_pg.md`, pulled from
Keyword Planner inside the live Ads account on 2026-09-08, and are **not yet approved by Dofrane**. Every
line below tagged `[PROV]` is written to an unapproved term and gets rewritten if the Tier 1 list changes.
Nothing tagged `[PROV]` carries a factual claim, so a keyword change is a copy edit, never a fact edit.

## 1. The problem the copy had to fix

The live page's H1 is **"Invest Smart, Live Better"**, printed twice. It pitches an investor audience on
the only page whose job is to convert a seller. That is finding **F-A01**. Around it sit 140 words of
builder boilerplate, no form, no county, and no statement of what the business actually does.

The rebuild inverts the address. Nobody on this page is being sold an opportunity. One person is being
told, plainly, what happens if they hand over a house they no longer want.

### 16. Why the CTA is not "Get my cash offer"

The `/sell` page on the `wholesale-sell-page` branch of `hod-web` uses **"Get my cash offer"**. This
package uses **"Tell us about the house"** on both pages. The reason is a fence, not taste: a button that
says "get my offer" promises an offer to anyone who clicks it, and the memo forbids promising an outcome
the pipeline cannot keep. It also sets the seller up to feel switched on when the first call is a
conversation, not a number. **This is a deliberate divergence from the branch. It is listed as D1 in
GATE.md and needs one word from Dofrane.**

## 3. Situation section, and the two situations deliberately left out

The five situations on the page describe **a property or a transaction**, never a person. That is the
fair-housing rule stated as a writing rule: condition, title status, lease status, code status, timing.

| On the page | Why it is safe |
|---|---|
| Needs work you will not pay for | Describes the building |
| Inherited and sitting | Describes title and occupancy status |
| Landlord, math stopped working | Describes the lease economics |
| Code or permit problems | Describes the file at the county |
| Moving on a date a listing cannot meet | Describes the calendar |

**Left out on purpose, and this is the important part:**

- **Pre-foreclosure and "behind on payments" are absent from the entire site.** Maryland's Protection of
  Homeowners in Foreclosure Act, Real Property § 7-301 et seq., defines a "foreclosure consultant" to
  include a person who solicits or contacts a homeowner and represents he can stop the foreclosure or
  obtain forbearance, or who systematically contacts owners of residences in default to offer services.
  `legal/OUTREACH_RULES.md` states the house position in one line: keep the message to buying houses for
  cash, as-is, and **drop rescue language entirely**. A "facing foreclosure?" block on a landing page is
  the single most common way a wholesaler in Maryland walks into that definition. It is not on the page
  and it should not be added without the memo owner clearing the exact wording.
- **Divorce is absent.** It is the standard fourth bullet in this category and it describes a person's
  marital status, not a property. Maryland fair housing law adds marital status as a protected class.
  We buy from anyone; the page has no reason to name who they are.

## 5. Two promises the page does make, and why they are keepable

1. **"Dofrane calls you back within one business day."** Carried over verbatim from the `/sell` page on
   the `wholesale-sell-page` branch so the two surfaces do not contradict each other. It is a contact
   commitment by one named person on an inbound volume currently at zero, so it is keepable. The branch

   one business day, sight unseen, is a price promise. Divergence D2 in GATE.md.
2. **"We will not call you again unless you ask us to."** This is already house law under the opt-out
   rules, so the page is publishing a rule the tracker already enforces rather than inventing one.

## 6. Design choices that are conversion choices

- **Dark brand frame, light form card.** The palette is `hod-web`'s exact token set, unchanged. The form
  sits on the ivory ground with ink text because the audience is on a phone, often an older one, often
  outdoors, and a form is where contrast stops being a style question.
- **No navigation.** One page, one action. There is nowhere else to go and nothing to browse.
- **The form is on the money page, not one click away.** The audit scored the live page 0/10 on
  conversion path. A click between the argument and the field is where this audience leaves.
- **`/sell` is `noindex,follow` and is not in the sitemap.** It is the paid landing page and carries the
  same form as `/`. Two indexable pages with the same form compete with each other and Google picks one.
  The money page is `/`; the ad page does not fight it. County pages will nest under `/sell/md/...` per
  the architecture in `wholesale-real-estate.md` §2 and will be indexable.

## 7. What is still weak, now that the data exists

The swipe file closes the old gap and opens three smaller, more specific ones.

1. **No Maryland source opened.** AgingCare and City-Data are national forums. Reddit blocks this
   agent outright, and Justia Answers and Avvo, which carry Maryland homeowner questions in the
   asker's own words, both returned 403 today. So the language is customer language but not
   *Montgomery and Prince George's* customer language. `SWIPE_FILE.md` §1 states this before the data
   rather than after it.
2. **The landlord bullet rests on one thread.** BiggerPockets has the volume and renders its threads
   client side, so nothing was capturable there. Four phrases from a single City-Data thread is the
   thinnest evidence on the page, and the bullet is written narrowly because of it.
3. **One bullet has no source at all.** The code-and-permit bullet is unsourced and labelled unsourced
   in §2b. It stays because the fence work makes it operationally useful, not because a seller said it.

The fix for all three is the same and it is now cheap: the two 403 sites are readable from a browser
session, and the first 20 inbound calls will supply Maryland phone language. Re-run and merge.
