Legal — read this before you launch

All three legal pages are DRAFT and carry a visible DRAFT banner. They were written from DA's real context, not boilerplate — but they encode 15 assumptions and 4 placeholders that an attorney and you must confirm. Full list in legal/ATTORNEY_REVIEW.md.

4 placeholders still in the pages:

{{CONTACT_EMAIL}} {{CONTACT_PHONE}} {{MAILING_ADDRESS}} {{ENTITY_LEGAL_NAME}} — these render literally on /privacy and /terms right now and must be filled before launch.

Top 5 for the attorney:

  1. Md. Real Prop. §10-715 — wholesaler notice/registration obligations for residential contracts.
  2. Entity: drafted as sole proprietorship pending the Maryland LLC — confirm which entity is contracting.
  3. Governing law flipped DC → Maryland — confirm venue.
  4. MODPA (Maryland Online Data Privacy Act) applicability to the lead form + analytics.
  5. "No fees / we pay closing costs" language vs Maryland transfer/recordation split (§14-104).

The honest red — is this ready for a first-time visitor?

One thing is still red, and it's the one a distressed seller feels first.

A seller in trouble often does not trust a web form. They want to see a phone number, or at least a name and an email, before they type their address and their situation into a box. The page offers neither. The form is the only channel. That is by design of ruling C01 (no phone yet) and the pending admin provisioning of a number and inbox — not an oversight, and I did not ship a fake placeholder to paper over it (verified: zero phone/email strings on the page).

So: the page is ready to look at and ready to submit to — but not yet ready to trust for a seller who needs a human. Clearing D5 (a real, monitored contact route) is the last thing between this and launch-ready, and it needs your ruling on C01, not more engineering.
